# Canada Housing Atlas frontend

The app opens to an interactive Canada map and a searchable city index.
The former chat-dashboard layout has been replaced. Design references,
research on X, access limitations, and geographic provenance are documented in
[FRONTEND-RESEARCH.md](FRONTEND-RESEARCH.md).

## Main flows

- Search cities with the search box or `/` shortcut. Sort by featured order,
  city name, lowest median, or highest median.
- Expand “Median price ceiling” to filter cities by their median asking price.
  This does not filter individual listings.
- Select a city from the map or index. Its detail tray shows the exact sample
  median, listing count, share under $1m, and bedroom medians.
- Drag the map, use the zoom buttons, scroll to zoom, or fit Canada /
  major metros. Close markers are decluttered; every city remains
  available in the index.
- Compare two cities using the workspace tab or a city's Compare control.
  Bedroom bars use a shared zero baseline across both cities, with exact prices.
- Open the Data view for a national listings explorer: search by address, city
  or FSA; filter by city (or all cities), province, beds, and minimum/maximum
  price; sort by lowest or highest price, newest sighting, or biggest discount;
  and page with “Load more”. Filters are reflected in the request, updates are
  debounced, and the table shows skeleton rows while it loads. Choosing a city
  here also selects it on the map.
- A city’s detail tray has an affordability tab: an illustrative mortgage
  calculator (purchase price, down payment, rate, amortization) that shows the
  monthly payment, the CMHC premium when down payment is under 20%, and the
  stress-test income estimate. A budget check takes a household income and
  shows the highest price that fits the 39% limit on those terms, and how many
  sampled listings in the city are at or below it. It follows the same
  arithmetic as the `mortgage_estimate` agent tool.
- The explorer's filters are mirrored into the URL (`dq`, `dprov`, `dbeds`,
  `dmin`, `dmax`, `dsort`, and `dscope=all` for all cities), so a filtered view
  survives a reload or can be shared. When a city has no listings in the chosen
  province, the empty state says so and offers “Show all cities”.
- Numbers count up when they change, city rows and listings rise in with a short
  stagger, and comparison bars grow in. All of this respects reduced motion.
- “Ask about this city” / “Ask about this comparison” opens the agent panel and
  fills a reviewable question. Enter sends; Shift+Enter adds a newline.
- The agent panel keeps conversation state when closed. New chat clears it.
  Dialogs trap focus and close with Escape. Reduced motion removes transitions.
- On a phone, use the Map / Cities switch. The comparison and agent flows remain
  available without a long stack of desktop panels.

The atlas uses the existing generated housing summary. The research agent,
API, guardrails, and listing dataset are unchanged. The offline demo is labeled
inside the agent panel and dataset information.

## Implementation

- `components/Workspace.tsx`: application state, explorer, detail tray,
  comparison, and modal entry points.
- `components/MapCanvas.tsx`: offline SVG basemap, marker placement, map camera,
  pan, zoom, and fit.
- `components/Chat.tsx`: existing SSE transport with the redesigned agent UI.
- `public/geography/canada.json`: 122 GeoNames city points and Natural Earth
  provinces / lakes, generated by `scripts/build_geography.py` (legacy
  `ontario.json` is unused).
- `public/fonts/`: self-hosted fonts and license files.

No new JavaScript dependency or map service account is required.
